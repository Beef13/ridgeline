The track ends with a fade-out beginning around 433s. Looping it as-is would
fade to silence and then jump back to a mid-level start, which is audible every
time. src/core/audio.js instead crossfades a second copy in at 432s over six
seconds and drops the outro entirely, so the loop point is inaudible.

Those two numbers live in MUSIC in src/core/audio.js. Swap the track and they
are the only things that need changing.
