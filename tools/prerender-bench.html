Palette lock

Every pixel snaps to its nearest neighbour in the fixed ramp below — imported art included.

Two-tone finds the two palette colours a pixel sits between and checkerboards them in proportion — a real blend, and the only one that holds a long gradient together. Offset just jitters the colour before snapping; it can only reach across gaps smaller than the jitter, which is why skies band.
